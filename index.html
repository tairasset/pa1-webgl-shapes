01   Find the floor

Move slowly in a well-lit area. A green ring means the surface is within 12° of horizontal. Red means too tilted.

02   Tap to place

The front turns towards you. Tap again to move the same speaker. Its size stays fixed.

03   Walk around it

Look at the speaker from another side. Clear removes it and releases its anchor. Stop ends the session.
